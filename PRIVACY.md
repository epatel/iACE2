# iACE privacy policy

*Last updated: 25 September 2026*

iACE is a Jupiter ACE emulator for iOS and Android, published by Memention AB. This policy covers the iACE app on the App Store and Google Play.

## Summary

iACE does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps these on your device only:

- the tapes you save with `SAVE` or import as `.TAP` files,
- your settings (sticky shift, volume, the last manual page),
- the state of the emulated computer, so it can resume where you left off.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app doesn't send it anywhere. It may be included in your device's own backups, such as iCloud or Google backup, as for any app.

On iOS, when updating from iACE 1.x, the app reads the tapes and settings that iACE 1.x kept on your device, once, to carry them over. That also happens only on the device.

## Network and permissions

iACE makes no network connections. On Android it requests no permissions; it doesn't even have internet access. Links in the app (the manual's web links, the source code, the manual PDF on phones) open in your browser only when you tap them. From then on, that website's own privacy policy applies.

When you import or export a tape, the system file picker or share sheet is used. You choose what to share and where.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
